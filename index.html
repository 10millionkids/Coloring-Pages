Good to know

Questions, answered clearly.

Clear expectations make digital products feel better from the first click.

What exactly do I receive?

You receive a digital bundle of printable colouring activities, educational resources, worksheets and busy-book activities.

Is this a physical book?

No. This is a digital product. Nothing will be shipped; you can download and print the pages.

What ages is it suitable for?

The collection is designed for a broad early-learning audience. Adults should choose pages that match the child’s age and interests.

Can I resell or share the files?

Use the files only within the displayed licence. Use your own descriptions, visuals and delivery links, and do not redistribute private download links.
